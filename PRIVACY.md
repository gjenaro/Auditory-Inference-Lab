# Auditory Inference Lab Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 29, 2026

Auditory Inference Lab is an experimental personal listening-performance application. This
policy describes the current application build represented by this repository.

## Information processed by the app

Auditory Inference Lab may process:

- microphone audio while Live SNR or the optional room-noise check is active;
- relative sound-level estimates derived from microphone audio;
- typed answers entered during listening tests;
- test names, trial responses, response times, thresholds, reliability indicators,
  language and voice preferences;
- the active audio route name and output channel count.
- selected filter candidates, bundled music track identifiers, randomized A/B
  assignments, no-difference responses, and inferred personal preference signals.

## How information is used

The information is used only to perform the requested measurements, display
results, calculate experimental personalized-audio filters, and maintain local
test history.

Microphone audio is analyzed in memory. Auditory Inference Lab does not intentionally record or
save raw microphone audio. In the ordinary speech-in-noise, volume, and noise-
profile modules, typed sentence responses are scored during the test and only the
resulting score is retained. The Predictive Listening module stores the typed
response with its stimulus, condition, score, timing, confidence, effort, and
replay count so the behavioral result can be audited. Avoid entering personal or
sensitive information as an answer or test name.

## Collection and sharing

The current app has no Auditory Inference Lab account, analytics SDK, advertising SDK, remote
database, or application-operated upload service. The app developer does not
collect or sell the locally stored test data through this build.

The Music Credits screen contains an external website link. If you follow it, the
destination website's privacy practices apply.

## Local storage

Test profiles, preferences, up to 100 test-history records, and up to 50 blinded
personalization runs are stored locally in the app's iOS container. iOS backups,
device-management tools, and system voice
services are controlled by Apple and the device owner, not by Auditory Inference Lab.

## Deletion

Use **Settings → Reset all test data and history** to clear the locally saved
profile, test records, and saved blind runs. Deleting the app normally removes its local container,
subject to any device backup retained under the owner's Apple settings.

## Health information and medical use

Results are uncalibrated and may be health-adjacent, but Auditory Inference Lab is not a clinical
audiogram, hearing aid, diagnostic service, or substitute for a qualified hearing
professional. Do not use it to make medical decisions.

## Children

Auditory Inference Lab is not specifically directed to children. The Girl and Boy speech options
are higher-pitch simulations generated from installed system voices; they are not
recordings of children.

## Changes

This policy must be updated if the app adds accounts, cloud synchronization,
telemetry, advertising, third-party data services, sharing, or materially different
processing.

## Contact

For privacy questions, use the issue tracker or contact method published with the
repository containing this policy.
